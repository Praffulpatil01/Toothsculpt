PATIENT GUIDANCE

Frequently asked questions.

Straightforward answers to common questions about dental visits, pain management, procedures, and sterilization.

We prioritize patient comfort at every phase. We use modern, gentle local anesthesia techniques, wait until the area is fully numb before starting, and explain what sensations to expect. You are in complete control — if you need a break at any point during your procedure, just raise your hand and we pause immediately.

Scheduled appointments are recommended so that we can dedicate unhurried chairside time to your examination. However, if you are experiencing severe tooth pain, accidental dental trauma, or sudden swelling, we welcome emergency walk-ins during working hours. Please call ahead at +91 79772 17906 so our team can prepare for your arrival.

We follow strict hospital-grade sterilization protocols. All dental burs, scaling tips, and stainless steel instruments undergo multi-stage ultrasonic chemical cleaning, are sealed inside color-changing indicator pouches, and autoclaved under monitored high-pressure steam. All patient drapes, suction tips, needles, and gloves are strictly single-use and discarded after each appointment.

Implant treatment typically involves two main phases: surgical placement of the biocompatible root post, followed by an osseointegration period (typically 8 to 12 weeks) during which the implant securely integrates with the jawbone. Once healed, a custom porcelain crown is permanently attached. Every patient receives a clear, stepwise timeline following their initial digital X-ray evaluation.

Yes, absolutely. We believe in complete financial transparency. After your clinical diagnosis and digital imaging, we present your treatment options with exact fee estimates, expected number of visits, and viable alternative solutions. No procedure begins without your informed consent.
